About us

Connecting Kenyans to trusted homes

QejaConnect links tenants with landlords and property owners directly, so finding a place to live is simple, transparent and safe.

Who we are

QejaConnect is a modern housing connection platform built in Kenya. We help people looking for a house, apartment, bedsitter or hostel connect with landlords and property owners without unnecessary middlemen.

The platform was co-founded by two university students, Eliya Sande of JKUAT and Peterwise Lusava of the University of Nairobi, who set out to make renting easier and more trustworthy for everyone.

What we offer

Find a home

Browse rentals, hostels and apartments across Kenya and filter by area, type and budget.

List your property

Landlords post vacancies directly and manage their tenants and bookings from one dashboard.

Talk directly

Chat with landlords and housemates, and reach our support team whenever you need help.

Our goal is a trusted rental ecosystem where tenants and landlords connect directly, safely and fairly.
